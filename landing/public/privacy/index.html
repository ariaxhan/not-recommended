Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Not Recommended does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is gathered.

Data stored locally

The extension stores the following items in chrome.storage.local, on your device only:

  • display settings;
  • question threads you create;
  • saved YouTube video titles, IDs, URLs, and save times;
  • the most recent intention sentence, if you entered one; and
  • the current Wikipedia detour set and its draw date.

This data stays in the browser profile where the extension is installed. Not Recommended has no account system, backend, analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry.

Network access

The content script runs on https://www.youtube.com/* to replace and simplify YouTube surfaces. The extension's background worker sends fixed, credential-free requests to https://en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php to retrieve random main-namespace encyclopedia articles.

It does not send saved videos, questions, settings, intentions, YouTube activity, or identifiers to Wikipedia. The worker does not accept arbitrary URLs from the page or content script, and it cannot be used as a general network proxy.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties. The extension contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or remote executable code. Its only outbound requests are the Wikipedia API calls described above.

Removing your data

Removing the extension through Chrome removes its local extension storage. Individual question threads and saved videos can also be removed directly from the extension's home screen.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date. The commitment above — no collection, no transmission of personal data — is the design of the extension, not a configurable setting.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be filed as issues on the GitHub repository.
